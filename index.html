Record what was observed, name the hazard and owner, then route it for supervisor review. Safety Issues and Good Catches document successful detection; incidents document injury or work stoppage

Record-type guide: Safety Issue = caught during pre-work inspection. Good Catch = caught during work. Incident = injury or work stoppage. These are independent records

How Safety 360 uses data

Rates are exposure-adjusted per 200,000 worked hours. Safety Issues and Good Catches are successful hazard detection; incidents are independent injury or work-stoppage outcomes. The app compares records by scope or hazard category, does not link cases, and prioritizes only unresolved follow-up, overdue closure, preventive-control gaps, and injury outcomes. Recommendations require human review and verified closure.
