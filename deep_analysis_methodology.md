# Safety 360: Deep Analysis Methodology

## Purpose

Safety 360 prioritizes leadership attention using exposure-adjusted safety outcomes, preventive-control execution, closure accountability, and work-condition context. It is decision support, not a causal or predictive model.

## Core measures

- Recordable rate = (REC + RECR + LTA) × 200,000 / total hours worked
- LTA rate = LTA × 200,000 / total hours worked
- Daily PTP and Weekly Focus Walk completion are calculated from expected submissions and actual submissions.
- Closure indicators include overdue safety issues, open/in-progress issues, Good Catch follow-ups required, and Good Catch follow-ups still open.

### Record interpretation

- **Safety Issues** are hazards successfully detected during pre-work inspection, before injury or work stoppage.
- **Good Catches** are hazards successfully detected during work, before injury or work stoppage.
- **Incidents** are independent events that resulted in injury or work stoppage.

Safety Issue, Good Catch, and incident records may be compared by project, period, or hazard category. They are not case-level links: the analysis does not assume a detected hazard later became an incident. Reporting volume is not a negative safety signal; closure measures evaluate unresolved follow-up separately.

## Analytical layers

### 1. Serious-risk mechanism

Incidents are evaluated by OSHA hazard category, hazard subcategory, body part injured, and project. The purpose is to identify serious-injury mechanisms such as falls or struck-by events, then target the relevant control review.

### 2. Control effectiveness

Projects are grouped by Daily PTP completion and Weekly Focus Walk completion thresholds. Their exposure-adjusted recordable rates are compared descriptively. These comparisons show observed relationships only; they do not show that a missed checklist caused an incident.

### 3. Overtime and work-condition context

Recordable rates are compared across overtime bands. Portfolio-level weather and work-activity profiles provide additional context for pre-task planning. Observed differences should be interpreted with hours worked, project mix, and sample size in mind.

### 4. Closure accountability

Safety 360 measures whether identified hazards become verified controls:

- Open Good Catch follow-ups / Good Catch follow-ups required
- Overdue issues / open or in-progress issues

This focuses leadership on hazard resolution, rather than reporting volume alone. “Overdue” and “open or in-progress” are different measures and are reported separately.

### 5. Fair peer benchmark

Project 360 compares a project with peers in the same project type and contract-size band. Metrics include recordable rate, Focus Walk completion, and overdue issues. This avoids a raw-count ranking that would disadvantage larger projects.

### 6. Safety Attention Index

The transparent index combines current project signals:

- Serious outcome: 100 points
- Closure backlog: up to 65 points
- Preventive-control gap: up to 36 points
- Overtime exposure and/or elevated rate: up to 20 points

The index is an action-prioritization rule. It is not a risk prediction score and must be reviewed by leaders with field context.

## Key limitations

- All data is synthetic and must not be interpreted as evidence about a real company, worker, or project.
- Association does not establish causation.
- Condition comparisons use the available portfolio data; they should guide questions and control reviews, not assign blame.
- Field Capture and Action Report routing are working browser prototypes; production use would require approved systems, role controls, and persistent storage.
